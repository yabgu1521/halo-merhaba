# Security and privacy

This foundation is not ready for real member data or payments. See SECURITY_THREAT_MODEL.md and release gates in MVP_SCOPE.md. Never commit secrets. NEXT_PUBLIC variables are browser-visible; service keys are server-only. Do not log credentials, messages, uploaded documents or signed URLs. Report issues privately to the operator; a security contact must be configured before launch.

Privacy, terms, cookie information and community rules require qualified review in the operating jurisdictions. No legal approval is implied. Identity and intentions cannot be guaranteed. Never send money to unverified contacts or share banking credentials/passwords. Be cautious about urgent money requests, use video communication before major commitments, meet safely and report suspicious behavior.
