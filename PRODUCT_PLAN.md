# TurkIndo Connect product plan

Status: planning and public-web foundation. No member workflow is released.

## Business and audience
Adults in Türkiye and Indonesia can seek friendship, language exchange, cultural connection, or serious relationships. Purposes remain independent; romance is never assumed. The founders are a Turkish–Indonesian married couple. Any additional founder claims require their approval.

The lifecycle is discover → connect → mutual match → communicate → build trust → relationship → voluntary marriage decision → Marriage Center → optional professional services.

## Commercial model
Configurable memberships and separately purchased translation, consultation, interpreter, and document-preparation services. Never sell access to people or promise marriage, visas, or government approval. Introduce services contextually and without repeated unsolicited prompts.

## Launch dependencies and decisions
Operating entity, tax/invoicing arrangements, service scope, refund rules, billing currencies, payment provider eligibility, data region, cross-border transfer review, retention schedule, moderation staffing and escalation procedures need owner/legal decisions before launch. They do not block local engineering.

## Metrics
Aggregate registrations, active members, country/purpose distribution, matches, conversations, membership conversions, orders and journeys. Define activity windows explicitly. Avoid third-party behavioral tracking by default. Never include message bodies or documents in analytics.

## Infrastructure categories
Web hosting, PostgreSQL/backups, private storage and egress, email delivery, malware scanning, observability, payment processing and staff operations. No unverified prices or revenue assumptions.
